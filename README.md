# E-Commerce Operations & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales, profitability, operational performance, customer behavior, returns, cancellations, and complaints.

The dashboard was developed using Microsoft Power BI and a synthetic e-commerce dataset containing 5,000 orders.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Monitor sales trends over time
- Evaluate category and regional performance
- Analyze warehouse and delivery performance
- Identify cancellation and return patterns
- Understand customer ratings and complaints
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profitability
- Generate data-driven business recommendations

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel / CSV
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence
- Business Analytics

## Dataset

The dataset contains 5,000 e-commerce orders covering the period from January 2025 to June 2026.

Key data areas include:

- Customer information
- Product and category information
- Sales and discounts
- Profitability
- Payment methods and status
- Warehouse operations
- Shipping and delivery
- Returns
- Cancellations
- Customer ratings
- Complaints

## Dashboard Structure

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Average Order Value
- On-Time Delivery Rate
- Sales trends
- Category performance
- Regional sales and profitability

### 2. Sales & Profitability

Analyzes:

- Sales and profit trends
- Category-level sales and profit
- Profit margin by category
- Regional profitability
- Discount versus profit relationship

### 3. Operations Performance

Analyzes:

- Warehouse processing time
- Delivery time by shipping method
- On-time versus delayed deliveries
- Cancellation rate by payment method
- Warehouse delivery performance

### 4. Customer & Returns

Analyzes:

- New versus returning customers
- Customer rating distribution
- Return rate by category
- Return reasons
- Complaints by category

### 5. Business Insights & Recommendations

Summarizes key findings from the analysis and provides business recommendations related to:

- Product quality
- Returns
- Customer complaints
- Delivery performance
- Discounts and profitability
- Operational efficiency

## Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately ₹42.52M.
- Total profit was approximately ₹7.71M.
- The overall on-time delivery rate was approximately 90.4%.
- Fashion had the highest return rate at approximately 12.8%.
- Product Quality was the most frequently recorded return reason.
- Electronics recorded the highest number of complaints.
- Customer ratings were concentrated around 4 and 5 stars.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower profitability in several order-level observations.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate product quality issues in categories with higher return rates.
- Analyze recurring complaint patterns in Electronics and Home & Kitchen.
- Investigate warehouse, regional, and shipping-related causes of delivery delays.
- Monitor discount levels and their impact on profit margins.
- Use customer ratings and complaints to identify areas for customer-experience improvement.
- Reduce avoidable returns, cancellations, and delivery delays while protecting profitability.
