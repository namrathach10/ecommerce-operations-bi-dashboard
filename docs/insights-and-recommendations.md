# Insights & Recommendations

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Financial Performance

The dashboard records approximately ₹42.52M in total sales and ₹7.71M in total profit across 5,000 orders.

This indicates a substantial volume of business activity with positive overall profitability.

---

### 2. Delivery Performance

The overall on-time delivery rate is approximately 90.4%.

Although the majority of completed orders are delivered on time, the remaining delayed orders represent an opportunity to investigate operational bottlenecks.

---

### 3. Product Returns

Fashion records the highest return rate at approximately 12.8%.

Product Quality is the most frequently recorded return reason, followed by Changed Mind and Not as Expected.

This suggests that product-level quality and expectation alignment should be investigated further.

---

### 4. Customer Complaints

Electronics records the highest number of complaints among the product categories.

Home & Kitchen and Fashion also contribute a significant number of complaints.

Further investigation into recurring complaint categories could help identify product or service issues.

---

### 5. Customer Ratings

Customer ratings are concentrated around 4 and 5 stars.

This indicates that a large proportion of recorded customer ratings are positive, while lower ratings can be examined alongside returns and complaints to identify potential service or product issues.

---

### 6. Discounts and Profitability

The dashboard includes an order-level scatter analysis comparing discount percentage with profit.

This allows the business to investigate whether higher discounts are associated with lower profitability at the individual-order level.

Discount strategies should therefore be evaluated together with profit margins rather than sales volume alone.

---

# Business Recommendations

## 1. Investigate Product Quality

Conduct a deeper analysis of products and categories with high return rates, particularly where Product Quality is frequently recorded as the return reason.

Potential actions include:

- Reviewing product specifications
- Identifying frequently returned products
- Monitoring supplier/product quality
- Improving product descriptions

---

## 2. Reduce Customer Complaints

Analyze complaint patterns within Electronics, Home & Kitchen, and Fashion.

Complaint categories can be segmented by:

- Product
- Category
- Region
- Customer type
- Shipping method

This can help identify recurring issues.

---

## 3. Improve Delivery Performance

Investigate delayed orders by:

- Warehouse
- Region
- Shipping method
- Processing time
- Delivery time

The objective should be to identify the operational factors associated with delivery delays.

---

## 4. Review Discount Strategy

Monitor the relationship between discount percentage and profit.

Discounts should be evaluated based on their effect on:

- Net sales
- Profit
- Profit margin
- Customer returns

This can help prevent excessive discounting from unnecessarily reducing profitability.

---

## 5. Reduce Avoidable Returns and Cancellations

Returns and cancellations should be analyzed alongside:

- Payment method
- Product category
- Customer type
- Shipping method
- Delivery performance

Reducing avoidable failures can improve both operational efficiency and customer experience.

---

# Management Takeaway

The dashboard indicates strong overall sales and profitability alongside a high on-time delivery rate.

The primary opportunities identified through the analysis are improving product quality, reducing customer complaints and returns, investigating delivery delays, and maintaining profitability while managing discounts.

The dashboard can therefore serve as a starting point for deeper operational and customer-level analysis.
