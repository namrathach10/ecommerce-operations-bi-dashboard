# Dashboard Overview

## Project Purpose

The E-Commerce Operations & Business Intelligence Dashboard was developed to provide a consolidated view of sales, profitability, operational efficiency, customer behavior, and return performance.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into interactive business intelligence views that can support operational monitoring and management decision-making.

---

## Dashboard Architecture

The dashboard consists of five analytical pages.

### Page 1 — Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance.

Key metrics:

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Profit
- Average Order Value
- On-Time Delivery Rate

Key visualizations:

- Sales Trend Over Time
- Sales by Category
- Sales & Profit by Region

Interactive filters:

- Date
- Region
- Category
- Customer Type

---

### Page 2 — Sales & Profitability

Focuses on financial and commercial performance.

Key analyses:

- Monthly sales and profit trends
- Sales and profit by category
- Profit margin by category
- Sales and profit by region
- Discount versus profit relationship

The discount-versus-profit scatter plot uses individual orders as the observation level to examine how discount levels relate to order-level profitability.

---

### Page 3 — Operations Performance

Focuses on fulfillment and logistics performance.

Key analyses:

- Average processing time by warehouse
- Average delivery time by shipping method
- On-time versus delayed deliveries
- Cancellation rate by payment method
- Warehouse delivery performance

This page helps identify operational areas that may require further investigation.

---

### Page 4 — Customer & Returns

Focuses on customer behavior and post-purchase performance.

Key analyses:

- Orders by customer type
- Customer rating distribution
- Return rate by category
- Return reasons
- Complaints by category

This page helps identify product and customer-experience patterns.

---

### Page 5 — Business Insights & Recommendations

Summarizes the major findings from the dashboard and translates them into practical business recommendations.

Areas covered include:

- Product quality
- Returns
- Customer complaints
- Delivery performance
- Discounts
- Profitability
- Operational efficiency

---

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive filtering and cross-filtering capabilities.

Users can analyze the data by:

- Date
- Region
- Product category
- Customer type
- Warehouse
- Shipping method
- Payment method
- Other dimensions available within individual visuals

Selecting elements within visualizations dynamically filters related dashboard content.

---

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a Business Intelligence workflow:

1. Data preparation using Power Query
2. Data type validation
3. KPI creation using DAX
4. Interactive visualization development
5. Operational and customer analysis
6. Insight identification
7. Business recommendation development
